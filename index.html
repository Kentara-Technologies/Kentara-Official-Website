FLEXIBLE PAYMENT

Build the software you need, pay on terms that fit.

Payment options that align software investments with your operational cash flow.

Kentara Standard

Traditional milestone-based payment structure.

  • 30% Upfront deposit upon kickoff
  • 40% At agreed project milestone
  • 30% Upon final completion and handover
Available for Eligible Clients

Kentara Flex

Spread your remaining development fee over time.

30% of the development fee upfront, with the remaining 70% paid over an agreed period after project completion and launch.

Third-party costs are paid separately and are not financed by Kentara. Terms and availability depend on project scope and approval.
