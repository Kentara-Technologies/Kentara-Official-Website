Kentara Standard

Traditional milestone-based development structure.

  • 30% Upfront deposit upon kickoff
  • 40% At agreed project milestone
  • 30% Upon final completion and handover
Recommended for Eligible Businesses

Kentara Flex

Spread your development balance post-launch.

30% of the development fee upfront, with the remaining development balance paid over an agreed period after project completion.

Third-party costs (such as cloud hosting, third-party APIs, and domain registration) are paid separately and are not financed by Kentara. Flex availability and terms depend on project scope and approval.

Strategic Partnerships

In selected cases, Kentara may consider strategic partnerships where there is strong mutual value and long-term business alignment.
