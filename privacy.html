Privacy notice.

How we handle your website enquiry and the information you share with us.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Who is responsible

Kentara Technologies is responsible for the personal information collected through this website and its project enquiry form. For privacy questions or requests, email info@kentaratech.com.

What you share with us

The enquiry form collects your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, service interest and project description. Estimated budget and payment preference are optional. We also receive information you choose to share in follow-up correspondence.

Please provide only what is needed for an initial discussion. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, sensitive personal information or private customer and employee records in the form.

Why we use it

We use your information to understand your request, assess how we can help, reply to you and prepare for a possible project discussion or proposal. Where applicable, this involves taking steps at your request before entering a contract. We also have a legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries and protecting the service from spam and abuse, balanced against your rights.

Your enquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing list. Choosing a budget or payment preference does not commit you to a purchase.

Who handles your information

Within Kentara, access to enquiry submissions is limited to the founder and CEO. We use the following providers to operate this website and receive enquiries:

  • Vercel: website hosting. Vercel privacy notice.
  • Formspree: form submission processing and storage. Formspree privacy policy.
  • Zoho Mail: enquiry notification emails and correspondence. Zoho privacy policy.

Providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and service logs. Our pages also load Google Fonts and third-party styling software, which makes requests from your browser to those providers. The website form does not save your entered details in browser local storage.

These providers may process information outside Nigeria. Their policies describe their processing and international arrangements; contact us if you would like information about the arrangements relevant to your enquiry. External services you choose to visit, such as WhatsApp or social platforms, have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep enquiries

For enquiries that do not become projects, our policy is to delete the enquiry and related correspondence from our Formspree inbox and Zoho mailbox within one month of the last contact. This includes enquiries where we receive no further response. We may retain relevant records longer where necessary to meet a legal obligation or address a dispute.

If an enquiry becomes a project, relevant information forms part of that project's records; its handling and retention will be addressed in the project agreement. Provider logs and backup copies may follow the provider's own retention and deletion schedules. Our one-month policy is not a promise that every provider backup is erased immediately.

Your project remains your business

Submitting an enquiry does not give us permission to publish your business name, project description or work. Any case study, screenshots or public project reference must be agreed with you separately and respect the confidentiality terms of the project.

Access to enquiries is restricted as described above. No online service can guarantee absolute security. If your project needs an NDA or a protected way to exchange confidential materials, please discuss that with us before sharing them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us about information you have provided, request access or correction, or request deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent. These rights depend on the processing and applicable law; we may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Email info@kentaratech.com to make a request or raise a concern. You can also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when our website enquiry practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. Separate project agreements cover the handling of client systems and project data.
